- task1.py (member function declaration and call, --ast, code generation):
  - A struct can declare functions after its fields. A field after a member function is an error at that field; a member function before the first field is an error at its fn; a member function can't share a name with a field or another member function.

  - read and print are reserved: errors as a variable, function, parameter, field or member function name, and for p.print(), read(5), print(), print(a, b) and print used as a value.
